VidGrid Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

VidGrid is a video player for Meta Quest headsets, made by Kaiserapps (Benjamin Kaiser). This policy explains what data the app uses and how. In short: VidGrid collects nothing, and your videos never leave your headset.

What data the app uses

  • Your video files. With your permission (the headset's "Videos" media permission), VidGrid reads the video files and folder names on your headset so it can list them and play them.
  • Your settings. VidGrid stores your chosen folders, layout, background, distance, padding, audio focus and stream count on the headset so they're the same next time. Playback positions are kept in memory only while the app is running.

How the data is used

Video files and folder names are used only to show and play your videos on the headset. Settings are used only to restore your preferences. All of it stays on your headset.

What the app does not do

VidGrid makes no network connections. It does not upload, share or sell any data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not collect personal information. It never modifies or deletes your videos.

Deleting your data

VidGrid keeps nothing anywhere except your headset. To delete what it stores, uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings › Apps › VidGrid › Storage › Clear data). To stop it reading your videos, turn off its Videos permission under Settings › Apps › VidGrid › Permissions. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete, but you're welcome to contact us with any questions.

Children

VidGrid is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@kaiserapps.com

For help using the app, see VidGrid support.
