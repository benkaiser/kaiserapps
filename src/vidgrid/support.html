VidGrid Support

Help for VidGrid on Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S.

Getting started

  1. Connect your headset to a computer with a USB-C cable and allow file access in the headset.
  2. Copy your videos into the headset's Movies or Download folder. Subfolders are fine; each one appears in VidGrid's folder list.
  3. Open VidGrid and allow access to your videos when asked.
  4. Press the menu button (☰) on the left controller, tick the folders you want, and close the menu.

VidGrid plays MP4, MKV, WebM and the other formats your headset supports, up to 4K at 60 fps.

Settings

  • Videos at once: how many videos play together, up to 16.
  • Audio focus: at 100% you hear only the video you're looking at; at 0% every video plays at full volume.
  • Distance and Padding: how far away the grid is and how much space sits between videos.
  • Seek step: how far each thumbstick push jumps.
  • 3D videos: play side-by-side and over-under files in 3D, or show them flat.
  • Start videos: start long videos part-way through, or always from the beginning.

Troubleshooting

My videos don't appear

Open the menu and press Rescan, which looks through the whole headset, including files that were only just copied over. Check that the folder is ticked in the list. Folders whose names start with a dot are hidden folders and are left unticked unless you tick them yourself.

VidGrid says it needs access to my videos

Press Allow video access in the menu. If you chose "Don't allow" before, press Open settings, or go to Settings › Apps › VidGrid › Permissions on the headset and allow Videos.

Playback stutters, or fewer videos play than I asked for

Very high-resolution files use more of the headset's video decoders. VidGrid lowers the number of streams automatically when it runs out, then tries again later. Lowering Videos at once or using 1080p copies of very large files keeps everything smooth.

A 3D video looks doubled or squashed

VidGrid spots 3D files from tags in the file or folder name: SBS or HSBS for side-by-side, and H-OU, H-TAB, or 3D together with OU or TAB for over-under. Very wide videos are treated as side-by-side. Rename the file to include the right tag, or switch 3D videos to 2D in the menu.

The grid is behind me or off to one side

Hold the Meta button to recenter, or press Recenter in the menu.
